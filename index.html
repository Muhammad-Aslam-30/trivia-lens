Trivia Lens

Version 2026-11-04.1

Point your phone at a trivia question on another screen. It reads the question and shows the answer, and says it out loud, without you touching anything.

Your key stays in this browser and goes only to Google’s Gemini API. Free-tier requests may be used by Google to improve its products, so keep it to trivia.
